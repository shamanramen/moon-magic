# Eclipses
Condensed from book/05, Eclipses, Nodes, and Cycles.

## new
what: A solar eclipse is a New Moon with the volume turned up to ten. The inscription gets autocorrected by the universe to match your destiny. It goes deeper, lasts longer, and cannot be easily overwritten.
how: Solar eclipses are for beginnings that feel fated, not forced. Do not force new starts on the exact day. Let it show you what it wants to show you.

## full
what: A lunar eclipse is a Full Moon with the volume turned up to ten. It detonates everything not meant for you. What was developing in the dark does not just become visible. It becomes undeniable.
how: Lunar eclipses are for releasing, finishing, and letting go. Sit with what it shows you for at least 48 hours before making major decisions.

## node
north: Near the North Node: increase. Where you are evolving toward. Hunger. Growth. The new territory.
south: Near the South Node: release. What you are outgrowing, what you have already mastered, the patterns you are being asked to move beyond.
contact: A natal planet within 5° of this eclipse degree. This is not general seasonal energy. It is a personal restructuring.
