# The 12 Houses
1: Identity, self-image, and how you present yourself to the world
2: Finances, self-worth, and the resources you build security from
3: Communication, local connections, siblings, and daily mental patterns
4: Home, family roots, emotional foundation, and private inner world
5: Creative expression, romance, joy, and what you do for the pure love of it
6: Daily routines, health, work habits, and acts of service
7: Partnerships, committed relationships, and how you meet others as equals
8: Shared resources, intimacy, transformation, and what you carry beneath the surface
9: Belief systems, higher learning, travel, and the philosophies you live by
10: Career, public reputation, legacy, and how the world receives your work
11: Communities, friendships, future visions, and the networks you belong to
12: Subconscious patterns, spiritual life, solitude, and what runs beneath your awareness
