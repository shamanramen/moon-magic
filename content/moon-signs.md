# Your Moon Sign
profile = the book's text (book/06). new / full = how this firmware meets a New or Full Moon. Draft lines, for Chad to edit.

## Aries
profile: Emotional independence and direct expression. You need challenge and quickly assert your feelings, often just as quickly moving past them. Your comfort comes from action and pioneering new emotional paths.
new: Your firmware wants to move first. Let it, but write the inscription before you sprint. Comfort is speed. Nourishment is direction.
full: When the light comes on, your reflex is to react and be done with it. Stay with the feeling one beat longer than feels natural.

## Taurus
profile: Emotional stability and sensory comfort are paramount. You thrive on security, routine, and tangible pleasures. Slow to change, your emotional firmware values steadfastness and a peaceful, harmonious environment.
new: Your firmware wants proof before it commits. Inscribe something small enough to start and solid enough to keep.
full: You will reach for the familiar to calm what this shows you. Notice whether it is nourishing you or just keeping things the same.

## Gemini
profile: Emotional curiosity and adaptability. You need mental stimulation and constant communication to feel secure. Restlessness can arise if your mind isn't engaged, seeking comfort in variety and intellectual connection.
new: Your firmware wants ten options. Inscribe one. Curiosity is the comfort. Commitment is the nourishment.
full: You may talk around what this shows you. Say it once, plainly, then stop explaining it.

## Cancer
profile: Deep emotional sensitivity and a strong need for belonging. You are nurturing, protective, and your emotional safety is tied to home and family. Your firmware is highly empathetic, seeking comfort in intimacy and security.
new: Your firmware asks "is it safe?" before "is it right?" Inscribe what you need, not only what keeps everyone held.
full: The shell goes up when the light comes on. Feel it fully, then check whether the old lesson still fits the room you're in.

## Leo
profile: Emotional warmth and a desire for appreciation. You need to shine and express yourself creatively and generously. Your comfort comes from feeling loved, admired, and having a central role in your emotional world.
new: Your firmware wants to be seen doing it. Inscribe the version you would do even if nobody clapped.
full: Being witnessed is the comfort. Witnessing yourself honestly is the nourishment. Start there.

## Virgo
profile: Emotional practicality and analytical processing. You seek order and perfection, finding comfort in helpfulness and efficient routines. Anxiety can arise from perceived imperfections, with emotional nourishment coming from service.
new: Your firmware wants the plan perfect before it begins. Inscribe the first useful step, not the finished system.
full: You will want to fix what this shows you right away. See it first. Fixing can wait until it's seen.

## Libra
profile: Emotional balance and harmony are crucial. You need partnership and fairness, often avoiding conflict. Your emotional firmware strives for equilibrium, finding comfort and nourishment in balanced relationships and aesthetic beauty.
new: Your firmware weighs everyone's comfort before your own. Inscribe the side of the scale that is yours.
full: Smoothing it over is the comfort. Naming the imbalance is the nourishment. You are allowed to tip the scale.

## Scorpio
profile: Emotional depth and intensity define you. You need control and profound connection, shying away from superficiality. Your firmware is transformative, seeking comfort in understanding hidden truths and experiencing deep emotional mergers.
new: Your firmware wants all of it or none of it. Inscribe the one thing you will go all the way with.
full: You already sense what's under the surface. The work is letting the feeling move through instead of holding it as leverage.

## Sagittarius
profile: Emotional expansion and freedom. You need adventure, knowledge, and an optimistic outlook. Your comfort comes from exploration and intellectual pursuit, often finding emotional nourishment in philosophical ideals and travel.
new: Your firmware wants the horizon. Inscribe the next real step toward it, not only the view.
full: Optimism can skip the hard part. Look at what this shows you before you turn it into a lesson.

## Capricorn
profile: Emotional structure and earned authority. You seek competence and respect, often suppressing feelings to maintain control. Your firmware values responsibility, finding comfort in achievement and building a secure legacy.
new: Your firmware wants it to count. Inscribe something you are building for yourself, not only for the record.
full: Control is the comfort. Letting yourself feel it without managing it is the nourishment. Both are strength.

## Aquarius
profile: Emotional vision and collective connection. You need intellectual freedom and unique self-expression, often detaching emotionally to maintain objectivity. Your comfort comes from innovation and contributing to a larger cause.
new: Your firmware thinks in systems and futures. Inscribe what you personally want inside the vision.
full: Stepping back to observe is the reflex. Step back in. This one is about you, not the pattern.

## Pisces
profile: Emotional dissolution and spiritual connection. You are highly empathetic and intuitive, needing to surrender to flow and compassion. Your firmware seeks comfort in artistic expression, dreams, and a deep connection to the mystical.
new: Your firmware feels everything at once. Inscribe one clear line so the feeling has somewhere to land.
full: You may absorb what isn't yours. Sort what this shows you into "mine" and "not mine" before you release it.
