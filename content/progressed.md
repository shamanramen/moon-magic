# Your Progressed Moon
Draft lines for Chad to edit. The natal Moon is the firmware. The progressed Moon is the update running on top of it: about two and a half years in each sign, about 29 years around the whole chart.

## signs
Aries: The update asks for courage. Your emotional body is learning to move first and feel it in motion.
Taurus: The update asks for steadiness. Your emotional body is learning that slow and solid is not the same as stuck.
Gemini: The update asks for curiosity. Your emotional body is learning to name what it feels out loud, and to ask instead of assume.
Cancer: The update asks for belonging. Your emotional body is learning where home actually is now, not where it used to be.
Leo: The update asks for visibility. Your emotional body is learning to be seen without performing.
Virgo: The update asks for honest maintenance. Your emotional body is learning that care is a daily practice, not a crisis response.
Libra: The update asks for balance. Your emotional body is learning to stop disappearing inside other people's comfort.
Scorpio: The update asks for depth. Your emotional body is learning to end things cleanly and let what is hidden be known.
Sagittarius: The update asks for room. Your emotional body is learning that freedom and commitment can live in the same place.
Capricorn: The update asks for structure. Your emotional body is learning that you can feel it fully and still hold the line.
Aquarius: The update asks for honesty about difference. Your emotional body is learning to stay connected without fitting in.
Pisces: The update asks for surrender. Your emotional body is learning to let go of what was never yours to carry.

## phases
new: Progressed New Moon phase. A new 30-year chapter is being inscribed. Name who you are becoming, even if it is not visible yet.
crescent: Progressed Crescent phase. The new chapter is pushing against the old one. Keep going when the past pulls you back.
firstQuarter: Progressed First Quarter phase. Crisis in action. Build the structure the new chapter needs and commit to it.
gibbous: Progressed Gibbous phase. Refinement. Adjust, edit, and get it ready to be seen.
full: Progressed Full Moon phase. What you started about fifteen years ago comes into full light. See it clearly, the results and the cost.
disseminating: Progressed Disseminating phase. Share what you learned. The harvest is meant to be passed on.
lastQuarter: Progressed Last Quarter phase. Crisis in belief. Drop what no longer fits the person you have become.
balsamic: Progressed Balsamic phase. The long release before the next chapter. Rest, finish, and let go of what is complete.
